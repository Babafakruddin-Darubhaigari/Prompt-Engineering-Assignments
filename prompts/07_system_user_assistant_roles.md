## 🧠 System, User & Assistant Promptings

### What it means
A good prompt is **clear, focused, and minimal**.  
It gives only the necessary information to complete a task.

---

### When to use it
- When you want **accurate and clean output**
- When tasks are **simple or moderately complex**
- When you want to avoid confusing the model

---

### 💡 Example

👉 Prompt:
```
You are a financial advisor.

I earn ₹40,000 per month and spend ₹25,000.
Calculate my monthly savings and suggest a simple plan.

Let's think step by step.
```

👉 What happens:
- Model understands the role
- Performs calculation
- Gives structured answer

---

### 🚀 Key Takeaway

👉 **Clarity > Complexity**

A simple and focused prompt gives better results than a long, confusing one.
