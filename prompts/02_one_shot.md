# One-Shot Prompting

## What it means

One-shot prompting means giving the AI **one example** before asking it to do a similar task.

The example helps the AI understand the pattern you want it to follow.

## When to use it

Use one-shot prompting when:

- the task needs a clear format
- you want the AI to copy a style or pattern
- zero-shot prompting gives unclear or inconsistent results

## Small example

If you want the AI to change informal text into professional text, you can show one example first:

**Example:**  
Informal: "Thanks a lot!"  
Professional: "Thank you very much."

Then you ask:

Informal: "Can you send it soon?"  
Professional:

The AI will usually understand that it should rewrite the sentence in a professional tone.

## Key takeaway

One good example can make the AI's answer more accurate and easier to control.
