# Assignment: Zero-shot Prompting

## Practical task
Write a zero-shot prompt that asks the model to summarize a short paragraph in one sentence.

## Rules
- Do not provide any examples in the prompt.
- Keep the prompt clear and concise.
- Use plain language and one complete sentence.

## What will be checked
- The prompt is a direct instruction without examples.
- The model can understand the task from the prompt alone.
- The task stays within the scope of a short paragraph summarization.
