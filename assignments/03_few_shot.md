# Assignment: Few-Shot Prompting

## Practical task
Write a few-shot prompt that asks the model to classify product reviews as positive, negative, or neutral.

## Rules
- Include 2-3 examples in the prompt.
- Keep the task focused on sentiment classification.
- Make the final instruction clear and follow the example pattern.

## What will be checked
- The prompt includes multiple examples (not just one).
- Each example demonstrates the correct classification.
- The final prompt asks for classification of a new review.
- The task is realistic and achievable with few-shot learning.
